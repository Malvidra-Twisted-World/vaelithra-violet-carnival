# Vaelithra - Violet Carnival

This part of the book is for DM's (Dungeon Master) eye only to see

## Game Setting

 This adventure is for level 5 4-6 adventurers.
 
 The adventure took place beyond time, in a demiplane that created by [[Belgruth]], a demon from Greed Domain of Dewanegara of the [[Little Sins]] Pantheon, a carnival owned by [[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Monster Family/Juval Garnix|Juval Garnix]] & [[Sylvia Garnix]] called [[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Locations/Violet Carnival|Violet Carnival]].
 
 Our Adventurer will be joining this adventure through an invite given to them at their own plane

## Running This Adventure

To run this adventure be sure to have any book as reference that suited your game style as a DM, however below is the recommended book for reference to have:

- Player Handbook 2014

### Hook Intro

The players would be introduced to the adventure with their own description on what was they doing before they found the invitation from the [[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Locations/Violet Carnival|Violet Carnival]]

> [!note] Read Aloud
> *You just finished your day and prepare to retire to the bed. But something caught your eyes, you see something glimmering at the corner of you eyes, and you see an enveloped, beautifully carved as the body of the enveloped looked like a glitter paper with violet color, you pick it up and inside you see a letter*
> *"Congratulations, you have been invited to the Violet Carnival. Let's the game begin!"*

This section can be use to introduced the player's characters whereabouts and time they discover the envelope.
The enveloped has been attached with Gate and Sleep spell, so after reading the envelope the character must succeed on a DC 17 Charisma Saving Throw and will be asleep immediately on a fail and the enveloped they holding would glow a violet light and teleport the character to the demiplane and  be prone at the front of the Carnival Gate.
If any of the character successful, they would gain a vision that can be described below.

> [!note] Read Aloud
> *You see a woman, wearing a white clothes unconscious, she was tied to a pole, her breath was weak but she's still looked alive. And then you're awake*
