# Part I: The Invitation

> *"Only those invited may enter."*

Wherever the characters are — a warm inn, a dusty road, the middle of an exhausting ordinary day — time is about to slow down for them, and no one else.

## The Violet Invitation

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Wherever you are — in a warm inn, on a dusty road, or in the middle of some tiring routine — time suddenly... slows.
>
> The sounds around you fade. Colors dim. As though the world itself is holding its breath.
>
> And there, in front of you, an envelope of deep violet simply appears.
>
> Not thrown. Not handed to you.
>
> It was already there.
>
> Its silver wax seal is carved with the symbol of an expressionless mask. The scent rising from it is sweet — too sweet.
>
> Strangely, no one around you seems to notice it at all.
>
> Only you.

***Running This Scene.*** Let each player react in their own way. Don't rush them, and don't force an emotion — just lean into the wrongness. If a character hesitates to open the envelope, it opens itself after about a minute of in-fiction time.

***A Note on Openings.*** This vision-and-envelope moment is the default opening for every character, every table. If you'd rather spend a few minutes first showing each character's life in the moment before the envelope arrives, see *Alternate Opening: Personalized Introductions* in *Running the Adventure*.

## The Living Letter

When the envelope opens, the ink inside moves on its own, the words seeming to rewrite themselves with every reading.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> *"With the deepest respect, the Violet Carnival invites you to the Night of Eternal Smiles."*
>
> The final letters shine bright violet — and the pulse of that light crawls straight into your eyes.

### Wisdom Saving Throw

Each character makes a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw**.

- **Failure.** The character loses consciousness completely, their body falling into a deceptive warmth.
- **Success.** The character stays half-aware — limbs heavy, thoughts distorted — but will remember more detail of the vision to come.

> ⚠️ **DM Note:** Every character enters the vision below regardless of the save's result. The save only affects the *clarity of the memory afterward*, never whether they see it at all.

## Vision of the Caged Woman

This is the adventure's core mystery hook.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> You see a woman with dark brown hair. Her clothes are torn, her skin pale, and her body is bound by threads of violet that move like living roots.
>
> She's trapped inside a crystal cage of deep violet — its cracks forming the pattern of a faceless mask.
>
> Her eyes meet yours.
>
> Afraid...
>
> But full of hope.

She whispers, barely audible:

> *"Juval... please..."*
> *"Whoever you are..."*
> *"Find me..."*

From within the shadows behind her, two enormous hands emerge — thick, maroon-skinned, black claws gleaming — and slowly reach for her.

> *"Smile..."*
> *(a foreign whisper echoes)*

The vision shatters like glass.

## The Portal Opens

As the characters come to, the violet envelope is not finished with them.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The violet letter folds itself. The paper becomes a thin frame, violet light spinning within it.
>
> A gateway opens.
>
> And from beyond it... you hear carnival music.

Through the portal, the **Violet Carnival in its prime** can be glimpsed:

- Glittering lights
- Colorful tents
- Children's laughter
- Beneath the music — a small, sinking sob

The portal pulls the characters through gently, without pain.

## Arrival at the Gate

> [!info] Read Aloud
> You stand before a great iron gate reading:
>
> **VIOLET CARNIVAL**
>
> The sky above is dusk-violet. Lanterns sway lazily. The air smells of sugar and metal.
>
> This carnival is... alive.
>
> But you can feel it — something is waiting.

In the distance, a **female clown** watches the party arrive, then withdraws into the crowd before anyone can approach her — this is the party's first, unremarked glimpse of **Varquin**.

## After the Vision: The Violet Ticket

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Awareness returns slowly.
>
> The sound of your own heartbeat fades, replaced by a faint waltz drifting from somewhere ahead.
>
> Small violet lights — like fireflies — dance in the air around you.
>
> And in your hand... the envelope has changed.

Each character now holds a **Violet Carnival Ticket**. Every ticket looks identical, yet feels deeply personal to its owner: lilac-colored, pulsing with a soft light; smelling of sweet bubblegum; elastic and warm to the touch, more like candy than paper. Silver letters shift constantly across its surface, most often settling on: *"Only those invited may enter."*

> ⚠️ **DM Note:** The ticket cannot be traded, copied, or destroyed by ordinary means. Magical attempts to duplicate it fail without any visible effect.

## The Gatekeeper Appears

> [!info] Read Aloud
> A stifled giggle drifts through the air.
>
> *"Hee... hee... hee..."*
>
> The carnival gate spins slowly on its own.
>
> From behind it emerges a tall, thin figure in a carnival guard's uniform. His face is painted stark white, brushed with faint violet. But the most striking thing about him is his top hat — it's grinning, wide and unmoving.

This is **Mr. Smiley Hat**, gatekeeper of the Violet Carnival. He bows deeply, his movements jerky and mechanical, and speaks in an overly sweet, overly rehearsed voice:

> *"Welcome, little stars~ The Violet Carnival has been waiting for you."*

His eyes never blink, even as his body moves faster than any human should.

## Ticket Validation: The Bite Ritual

Mr. Smiley Hat extends an open hand.

> *"Tickets, please~ May I see~?"*

When a character hands theirs over —

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Without warning, he leans in close.
>
> His teeth press into the ticket — not like someone eating, but like something testing the quality of a living thing.
>
> The ticket trembles... then goes still.

The ticket is not damaged; a small bite mark remains, gleaming like a hologram, and the ticket now feels colder and heavier. Mr. Smiley Hat licks his lips slowly.

> *"Ahhh~ delicious... Valid."*

He returns the ticket.

> *"You may enter."*

## The Violet Ticket: Bite Mark System

Every Violet Carnival Ticket carries **5 bite marks** when first received.

***Basic Rules.*** One bite grants access to one attraction, forbidden area, or feature. A bite is consumed on **entry**, not on exit, and its mark fades once used.

***Bypassing a Bite.*** Some locations can be entered without spending a bite, through: solving a puzzle, finding a hidden path, completing a favor for an NPC, or careful environmental investigation.

***Running Out of Bites.*** A drained ticket turns pale and cold. The character can no longer enter new attractions normally — the only paths forward are negotiation, trickery, or a hidden quest.

> ⚠️ **DM Tip:** The Bite System is a resource of psychological pressure, not a punishment. Never let a party soft-lock with no way forward.

## Words of Warning

Mr. Smiley Hat steps aside, opening the path.

> *"Five bites remaining~ Every attraction asks for a taste."*

He gives a small chuckle.

> *"Secrets may open... or you may be swallowed instead."*

Then, in an almost soothing tone:

> *"But don't worry."*
> *"You're safe."*
> *"...For now."*

## Entering the Violet Carnival

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The moment you cross the gate, the world changes.
>
> The air turns warm, like an autumn afternoon.
>
> The scent of caramel, lavender... and something faintly metallic.
>
> A music box melody plays sweetly, broken now and then by a sour, wrong note.
>
> Violet lights dance at every corner.
>
> And in the distance...
>
> a masked figure stands frozen.

A **Gluttonette** — its mask cracked, its expression empty. When one of the characters looks directly at it, it spins like a wind-up doll and vanishes into the mist.

## Ending the Chapter

**What the Party Knows.** The Violet Carnival has rules, and a price. Their tickets are a precious, limited resource. They are not alone here — something has been watching them from the very start.

The next Part carries the party deeper into the carnival proper, where they'll meet Juval and Varquin at the true gate, and face the first two attractions: the Carousel and the Giant Snail Race. Continue to *Part II: Carousel and Snail Race*.
