# Part V: The Invitation to Smile, Fulfilled

> *"Time has been disturbed — and it remembers who dared to step into its carnival."*

With Belgruth gone and Sylvia freed, the Violet Carnival breathes its last performance: a quiet reunion, a confession long overdue, and a gift that hints at a much larger story still waiting to unfold.

## Aftermath on the Stage

> [!info] Read Aloud
> The dying spotlight above the stage flickers once, then steadies into something softer, warmer. The violet smoke of Belgruth's death drifts upward and away, taking with it a weight you hadn't fully realized you'd been carrying.

**Varquin** recovers — whether she was ever unconscious from her intervention or simply worn thin by the fight, she rises slowly, leaning on the ruined stage for support. For the first time since the party met her, her smile isn't a performance.

> *"You did it."*
>
> *"I mean that. Truly. Thank you."*

***Roleplaying Varquin, Unmasked.*** Gone is the deflecting, riddling showwoman from earlier chapters. Varquin speaks plainly now, tired but relieved, treating the party as equals rather than pieces in a game she was running.

## Reunion

Across the stage, **Juval** finally crosses the threshold he couldn't pass before — the binding that held him at the gate breaks the instant Belgruth falls. He crosses the ruined stage at a run.

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Juval reaches Sylvia and drops to his knees beside her, gathering her into his arms as though afraid she might vanish if he lets go. She's weak, barely able to lift her head, but her hand finds his face, and she smiles — the first real smile this carnival has seen in a very long time.
>
> Neither of them says anything for a long moment. They don't need to.

Let this moment breathe. If any party member has grown close to Juval or Sylvia over the course of the adventure, give them a chance to speak with the couple once the initial reunion has settled.

## Varquin's Confession

Once the moment passes, Varquin gestures to the cracked mask she still holds — the same one she pressed to her chest during the battle, now visibly fractured, its violet glow dimmed to a faint pulse.

> *"I owe you the truth, at least. Or as much of it as I can give."*
>
> *"Belgruth built this place — this whole demiplane — somewhere beyond the reach of ordinary gods and ordinary heroes. I couldn't act openly. Whatever bound me here, it wouldn't allow that."*
>
> *"So I did what I could. I gave Juval this mask — a fragment of my own power — and let him carry pieces of me into the carnival where I couldn't go myself. Every clue, every puzzle, every 'accident' that led you here... that was the two of us, working around the edges of a cage neither of us built."*

Varquin doesn't explain further — not what she is, not why she was bound here, not who or what placed the demiplane beyond ordinary reach in the first place. If asked directly what she is, she only offers a tired, knowing smile.

> *"Something old. Something that's done this before. That's enough for tonight."*

***Running This Reveal.*** Don't over-explain. Varquin is a **human Vaelithra** — a truth players of earlier Vaelithra adventures may already suspect from her card-magic and her impossible knowledge, but one she never confirms outright here. Leave the door open for a future adventure to answer the rest.

## A Gift

Before the carnival's sky finishes its slow shift toward dawn, Varquin presses something small into the hands of whichever character she feels closest to (or, if the DM prefers, the party's nominal leader).

> [!info] Read Aloud
> She presses a necklace into your palm — a silver chain holding a pendant shaped like two things at once: a small, delicate mask, and an open book without pages. The silver catches the light strangely, flashing faint violet at the edges, as though something inside it is still watching.
>
> *"Aegis Vaeil: Echo of Chronos,"* she says. *"Keep it close. There's a bigger shadow than Belgruth out there — a broken thread in the tapestry of time. You'll understand, someday."*

See *Appendix A: Magic Items* for the full description of the **Aegis Vaeil: Echo of Chronos**. This item carries no immediate mechanical benefit — it exists as a narrative anchor and a hook for future adventures within the Vaelithra saga.

## The Carnival Fades

> [!info] Read Aloud
> As the sky above the tent finishes its shift from violet to the pale gold of an ordinary dawn, the ground beneath your feet begins to blur, colors bleeding into one another like a painting left out in the rain. Varquin, Juval, and Sylvia grow faint, their voices swallowed by a rising wind.
>
> And then — silence. And your own world, rushing back in around you.

The party is pulled back to their point of origin, at the exact moment they were taken.

## What Is Remembered

Each character makes a **DC 12 Wisdom saving throw**.

- **Success.** The character retains full, clear memory of everything that happened in the Violet Carnival.
- **Failure.** The memories blur like a fading dream. The character knows *something* happened — and that the pendant, if they carry it, matters — but the details slip away no matter how hard they try to hold on.

***DM Note.*** Regardless of the save's result, the physical items the party recovered (including the Aegis Vaeil: Echo of Chronos, if given) remain with them, real and tangible, whether or not their memories of *earning* them survive intact.

## Closing

> [!info] Read Aloud
> Wherever you find yourselves now, the world has resumed exactly as it was — no time lost, no one else the wiser. Only you know what happened. Only you carry what's left of it.
>
> Time has been disturbed — and it remembers who dared to step into its carnival.

## Ending the Adventure

**What the Party Achieved.** Sylvia is free. Belgruth has been claimed by the Inevitables and will answer for its crimes beyond any mortal court. Varquin's true nature remains only half-revealed, and the Aegis Vaeil: Echo of Chronos now sits among the party's possessions — a quiet promise that this was not the last time the tapestry of time will call on them.

This concludes *Vaelithra III: Violet Carnival*. For guidance on running the adventure as a whole, see *Violet Carnival - Running the Adventure*; for full write-ups of every magic item and creature featured, see *Appendix A: Magic Items* and *Appendix B: Creatures*.
