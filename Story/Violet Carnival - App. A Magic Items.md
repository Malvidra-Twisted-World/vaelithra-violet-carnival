# Appendix A: Magic Items

This appendix contains every magic item featured in *Vaelithra III: Violet Carnival*, from the ticket every character carries from the moment they arrive, to the campaign-hook artifact that closes the adventure.

> [!loot] Violet Carnival Ticket
> *Wondrous Item, Uncommon (no attunement required)*
>
> Every character receives one of these tickets in *Part I: The Invitation*, the moment their invitation letter transforms upon crossing into the carnival. Lilac-colored and pulsing with a faint inner light, it smells faintly of bubblegum and feels warm and elastic to the touch, more like candy than paper. Silver text shifts constantly across its surface, most often settling on the words *"Only those invited may enter."*
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> The ticket cannot be traded, copied, magically duplicated, or destroyed by ordinary means. It carries **5 bite marks**, consumed by **Mr. Smiley Hat** one at a time as the party enters attractions throughout the carnival (see *Part I: The Invitation* for the full Bite System). Once drained, the ticket turns pale and cold, and grants no further automatic entry — though many of the carnival's attractions can still be reached through puzzles, hidden paths, or NPC favors instead.

> [!loot] Faded Keepsake
> *Wondrous Item, Common (no attunement required)*
>
> Awarded to whichever character's snail crosses the finish line first during the Giant Snail Race in *Part II: Carousel and Snail Race*. A dull, unremarkable glass marble at first glance, bearing a faint, nearly-worn-away symbol matching the Archive of Vaeil.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> A character who studies the marble and succeeds on a **DC 15 Intelligence (Arcana or History)** check hears faint whisper-fragments trapped within it — disjointed impressions of Gluttonettes and a curse that fades memory, hinting at Belgruth's true nature well before the party confronts it directly. Presenting this item to **Varquin** during *Part III: Echoes and Fortunes* changes the shape of her tarot reading (see that chapter for details), giving her one further piece of guidance she wouldn't otherwise offer.

> [!loot] Key Pendant
> *Wondrous Item, Common (no attunement required)*
>
> Rises from the black water of the Tunnel of Echoing Memories if the party resolves at least three of its four memory fragments in *Part III: Echoes and Fortunes*. A small silver pendant shaped like an ornate key, humming faintly with a melody only its bearer can hear clearly.
>
> ___
>
> ### Effect
> A bearer who succeeds on a **DC 12 Wisdom (Insight)** check while holding the pendant and concentrating can recall its melody well enough to hum or play it. This melody is the key to the Music Box puzzle at the heart of the Violet Carnival, and playing it correctly for **Juval** at the Dalang's Final Gate (*Part IV: Into the Big Top*) grants advantage on the party's next Persuasion attempt to reach him emotionally.

> [!loot] Aegis Vaeil: Echo of Chronos
> *Wondrous Item, Legendary (requires attunement)*
>
> Given by **Varquin** to the party in the adventure's final scene, *Part V: The Invitation to Smile, Fulfilled*, as the carnival fades around them. A silver necklace whose pendant merges two shapes into one — a small, delicate mask, and an open book without pages. Its silver surface catches light strangely, flashing faint violet at the edges, as though something within it is still watching.
>
> ___
>
> ### A Fragment of a Larger Story
> This item carries no combat statistics and grants no mechanical benefit at the close of this adventure. It exists as a narrative anchor: proof that the party's encounter with a Vaelithra fragment was real, and a promise of stories still to come. Varquin's parting words hint at "a bigger shadow... a broken thread in the tapestry of time" — DMs running future Vaelithra saga adventures should treat this pendant as a hook connecting back to *Violet Carnival* whenever it becomes relevant.

## Carousel Rewards

Once the party solves all four unicorn-name pairs and breaks the violet barrier in *Part II: Carousel and Snail Race*, a hidden compartment opens beneath the carousel's central pole. Roll or choose one item per character from the table below.

| d10 | Item | Description |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Bubblegum-Scented Vial** | A small glass vial of pink liquid, smelling exactly like the party's own tickets. Uncorking it releases a burst of sickly-sweet mist; a creature that inhales it must succeed on a DC 12 Constitution saving throw or have disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws for 1 minute. |
| 2 | **Cracked Mirror Shard** | A palm-sized shard from a carnival mirror. Holding it up to a creature and succeeding on a DC 13 Wisdom (Insight) check reveals whether that creature is currently under an illusion or charm effect. |
| 3 | **Frostbitten Ribbon** | A silk ribbon perpetually cold to the touch. Tied around a weapon as a bonus action, the weapon's next hit deals an extra 1d4 cold damage. |
| 4 | **Wind-Up Key** | An ornate brass key that fits no lock the party has found. When turned, it grants advantage on the next Animal Handling check made against a construct or clockwork creature within 1 minute. |
| 5 | **Painted Mask Fragment** | A shard of porcelain from a broken Gluttonette mask. While holding it, a creature has advantage on saving throws against being frightened by masked or fiendish creatures. |
| 6 | **Violet Confetti Pouch** | Thrown as an action, the pouch bursts into a 10-foot-radius cloud of harmless, blinding-bright confetti; creatures within must succeed on a DC 12 Dexterity saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn. |
| 7 | **Tiny Music Box** | A miniature, functional music box playing a fragment of the carnival's theme. When wound and opened within 30 feet of a Gluttonette, it has disadvantage on its first attack roll that turn, distracted by the familiar melody. |
| 8 | **Carnival Token (Blank)** | An otherwise ordinary brass token, engraved with nothing. Presented to any carnival NPC, it is treated as proof of "having played fair" — useful for talking down suspicious attraction keepers. |
| 9 | **Faded Photograph** | A photograph of the carnival in its prime, showing Juval and Sylvia smiling together, unaware they're being watched. Studying it grants insight (DM's discretion) into their relationship before Belgruth's arrival. |
| 10 | **Whisper-in-a-Bottle** | A corked bottle containing a faint, swirling mist. Uncorking it releases a fragment of an old carnival announcement, read aloud by the DM — a small piece of flavor and lore, harmless and non-mechanical. |
