### **Sylvia Garnix, The Beacon of Fading Hope**
*(Half-Elf Cleric, Tersandra Belgruth)*

> Sylvia Garnix adalah pendeta dari dewa cahaya atau kehidupan, dan istri tercinta Juval. Dulunya ia adalah pilar kebaikan dan harapan di Violet Carnival, membawa sukacita dan penyembuhan. Namun, kini ia terbelenggu oleh Belgruth, kekuatannya terkuras dan jiwanya terperangkap. Statblock ini menggambarkan kemampuannya sebelum ia disandra, atau saat ia berhasil diselamatkan dan mendapatkan kembali kekuatannya di tengah pertarungan.

*Medium humanoid (half-elf), lawful good*
___
**Armor Class** 16 (scale mail, shield)
**Hit Points** 68 (12d8 + 12)
**Speed** 30 ft.
___
| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 10 (+0) | 12 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 14 (+2) |
___
**Saving Throws** Wis +7, Cha +5
**Skills** Insight +7, Medicine +7, Persuasion +5
**Damage Resistances** None
**Condition Immunities** None
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Elvish
**Challenge** 4 (1,100 XP)
___

***Fey Ancestry.*** Sylvia has advantage on saving throws against being charmed, and magic can't put her to sleep.

***Spellcasting.*** Sylvia is an 8th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save DC 15, +7 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following cleric spells prepared:

Cantrips (at will): *guidance*, *light*, *sacred flame*, *thaumaturgy*
1st level (4 slots): *bless*, *cure wounds*, *guiding bolt*, *shield of faith*
2nd level (3 slots): *aid*, *hold person*, *spiritual weapon*
3rd level (3 slots): *beacon of hope*, *dispel magic*, *spirit guardians*
4th level (2 slots): *death ward*, *guardian of faith*

***Disciple of Life.*** Whenever Sylvia uses a spell of 1st level or higher to restore hit points to a creature, the creature regains additional hit points equal to 2 + the spell's level.

***Channel Divinity: Preserve Life (1/Short or Long Rest).*** As an action, Sylvia presents her holy symbol and uses her Channel Divinity. She chooses any creatures within 30 feet of her, and divides 40 hit points among them. This feature can't restore a creature to more than half of its hit point maximum.

#### Actions
***Mace (Melee Weapon Attack).*** +3 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 4 (1d6 + 1) bludgeoning damage.
