# Statblocks

---

### **Catatan DM: Beragamnya Gluttonette**

Gluttonette adalah makhluk aneh yang tercipta dari memori dan emosi yang dicuri oleh Belgruth. Mereka muncul dalam berbagai bentuk dan ukuran, mencerminkan fragmen jiwa yang telah mereka lahap. Meskipun semua Gluttonette ini memiliki Challenge Rating 1, mereka memiliki spesialisasi dan kekuatan yang berbeda. Pemain mungkin akan menghadapi campuran dari tipe-tipe ini dalam satu *encounter*.

---

## **Gluttonette T1 (The Ravenous Shadow)**
*(Jenis Gluttonette Terkuat)*

*Medium construct, chaotic evil*
___
**Armor Class** 13 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 85 (10d8 + 40)
**Speed** 30 ft.
___
| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) | 18 (+4) | 6 (-2)  | 10 (+0) | 8 (-1)  |
___
**Skills** Perception +2
**Damage Resistances** Psychic
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, Frightened, Exhaustion, Poisoned
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
**Languages** understands the languages it knew in life but can't speak
**Challenge** 1 (200 XP)
___

***Memory Drain.*** When the Gluttonette T1 hits a creature with a melee attack, the target must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or have disadvantage on its next attack roll or ability check until the end of its next turn.

#### Actions
***Multiattack.*** The Gluttonette T1 makes two Slam attacks.

***Slam (Melee Weapon Attack).*** +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 9 (1d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) psychic damage.

***Echoing Wail (Recharge 6).*** The Gluttonette T1 emits a mournful wail. Each creature within 15 feet of it must make a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, a creature is frightened until the end of its next turn.
