## Belgruth Information

Belgruth adalah seorang Iblis dari pantheon **[[Little Sins|Little Sins]]**.
Ia berhasil menemukan cara untuk memakan jiwa lebih banyak dengan menggunakan **[[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Monster Family/Juval Garnix]]** sebagai pemilik dari **[[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Locations/Violet Carnival|Violet Carnival]]** dengan menjanjikan **[[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Monster Family/Juval Garnix|Juval]]** agar karnaval miliknya dan istrinya, **[[Sylvia Garnix]]** dapat banyak pengunjung
mengajukan satu syarat bahwa jangan pernah mempertanyakan kunjungannya apabila sewaktu-waktu Belghruth datang ke **[[D&D/Original Adventures/Vaelithra/3. Violet Carnival/Locations/Violet Carnival|Violet Carnival]]**.
Belgruth menculik pengunjung karnaval satu per satu, memakan jiwa mereka dan mengubahnya menjadi Marionette.

### **Catatan DM: Transformasi Belgruth**

Pertarungan melawan Belgruth akan berlangsung dalam dua fase. Saat Belgruth (Humanoid Form) dikalahkan (HP-nya mencapai 0), ia tidak mati, melainkan akan meledak dengan energi busuk dan segera bertransformasi menjadi Belgruth (Demon Form) di tempat yang sama. Dia akan transformasi ke Demon Form-nya dengan HP 50-40% (atau tergantung kondisi saat transformasi, dihitung dengan persenan), menandai dimulainya fase kedua yang jauh lebih berbahaya.

## **Belgruth, The Grand Puppeteer**
*(Fase 1: Bentuk Humanoid)*

*Medium fiend (demon), chaotic evil*
___
**Armor Class** 16 (glamored studded leather)
**Hit Points** 170 (20d8 + 80)
**Speed** 30 ft.
___

| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 18 (+4) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) |
___
**Saving Throws** Con +7, Int +7, Wis +6, Cha +8
**Skills** Deception +8, Insight +6, Performance +8, Persuasion +8
**Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** Poison
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
**Senses** Truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 13
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 8 (3,900 XP)
___

***Magic Resistance.*** Belgruth has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Aura of Temptation (15 ft. radius).*** Any creature hostile to Belgruth that starts its turn in this aura must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature has disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of its next turn. On a successful save, the creature is immune to Belgruth's Aura of Temptation for the next 24 hours.

***Grand Puppeteer.*** Belgruth can use a bonus action to command up to three Gluttonettes within 60 feet of him that he can see. Each Gluttonette can move up to its speed and make one attack.

***Innate Spellcasting.*** Belgruth's innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save DC 16). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:

At will: *detect thoughts, disguise self, minor illusion, suggestion*
3/day each: *charm person, enthrall, hold person, phantasmal force, psychic lance*
1/day each: *dominate person, mass suggestion*

***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** If Belgruth fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

#### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Belgruth makes two Draining Touch attacks.

***Draining Touch (Melee Spell Attack).*** +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. Hit: 12 (2d6 + 5) psychic damage, and Belgruth regains hit points equal to the damage dealt.

***Puppeteer's Gaze (Recharge 5-6).*** Belgruth targets one creature he can see within 60 feet of him. The target must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw or take 21 (6d6) psychic damage and be frightened for 1 minute. The frightened creature can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.

#### Legendary Actions
Belgruth can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Belgruth regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

*   **Move.** Belgruth moves up to his speed.
*   **Command Gluttonette (1 action).** Belgruth commands one Gluttonette within 60 feet of him to make one attack.
*   **Whisper of Doubt (2 actions).** One creature Belgruth can see within 60 feet must make a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature has disadvantage on the next attack roll or ability check it makes.

## **Belgruth, The Ravenous Maw**
*(Fase 2: Bentuk Iblis)*

*Large fiend (demon), chaotic evil*
___
**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 220 (20d10 + 110)
**Speed** 40 ft., climb 30 ft.
___
| STR     | DEX     | CON     | INT     | WIS     | CHA     |
|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|:--------|
| 22 (+6) | 10 (+0) | 24 (+7) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 20 (+5) |
___
**Saving Throws** Str +10, Con +11, Wis +5, Cha +9
**Skills** Athletics +10, Intimidation +9, Perception +5
**Damage Resistances** Cold, Fire, Lightning; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks
**Damage Immunities** Poison
**Condition Immunities** Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Restrained
**Senses** Truesight 120 ft., passive Perception 15
**Languages** Abyssal, Common, Telepathy 120 ft.
**Challenge** 10 (5,900 XP)
___

***Magic Resistance.*** Belgruth has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.

***Aura of Despair (30 ft. radius).*** Any hostile creature that starts its turn in this aura must make a DC 17 Wisdom saving throw. On a failed save, the creature is frightened until the start of its next turn. If a creature's saving throw is successful, the creature is immune to Belgruth's Aura of Despair for the next 24 hours.

***Devouring Maw.*** Belgruth's attacks deal an extra 3 (1d6) psychic damage if the target is frightened.

***Legendary Resistance (2/Day).*** If Belgruth fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

***Soul Consumption.*** When a humanoid within 30 feet of Belgruth is reduced to 0 hit points, Belgruth gains 20 temporary hit points.

#### Actions
***Multiattack.*** Belgruth makes three attacks: one with his Claws, one with his Tongue Lash, and one with his Gnashing Bite.

***Claw (Melee Weapon Attack).*** +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. Hit: 15 (2d8 + 6) slashing damage.

***Tongue Lash (Melee Weapon Attack).*** +10 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. Hit: 13 (2d6 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 7 (2d6) acid damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it is grappled (escape DC 18). Until this grapple ends, Belgruth can't use his Tongue Lash on another target.

***Gnashing Bite (Melee Weapon Attack).*** +10 to hit, reach 5 ft., one grappled creature. Hit: 19 (3d8 + 6) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) psychic damage. If this damage reduces the target to 0 hit points, the target is swallowed. While swallowed, the creature is blinded and restrained, it has total cover against attacks and other effects outside Belgruth, and it takes 10 (3d6) acid damage at the start of each of Belgruth's turns. If Belgruth takes 30 or more damage on a single turn from a creature inside it, Belgruth must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw at the end of that turn or regurgitate all swallowed creatures, which fall prone in a space next to Belgruth. If Belgruth dies, a swallowed creature is no longer restrained by him and can escape from the corpse using 15 feet of movement, exiting prone.

***Belly Burst (Recharge 4-6).*** Belgruth violently convulses, causing spectral figures to burst from his translucent belly in a 20-foot radius sphere centered on him. Each creature in that area must make a DC 17 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 28 (8d6) psychic damage and is pushed 10 feet away. On a successful save, a creature takes half as much damage and isn't pushed.

#### Legendary Actions
Belgruth can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below. Only one legendary action option can be used at a time and only at the end of another creature's turn. Belgruth regains spent legendary actions at the start of his turn.

*   **Move.** Belgruth moves up to his speed.
*   **Slam (1 action).** Belgruth makes one Claw attack.
*   **Gorge (2 actions).** Belgruth uses his Gnashing Bite.
