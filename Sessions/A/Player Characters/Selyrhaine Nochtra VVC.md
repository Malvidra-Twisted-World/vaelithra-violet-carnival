# Backstory

Born beneath the same unsetting moon as her twin, Selyrhaine Nochtra entered the world restless and unyielding. Where Xilthrae listened, Selyrhaine acted. She sensed decay long before others acknowledged it, and her magic answered that instinct with shadow—sharp, volatile, and final.

Feared by the elders of House Nochtra, Selyrhaine was labeled dangerous not for what she destroyed, but for how quickly she chose to end what could not be saved. Only Xilthrae stood beside her, tempering her force without ever silencing it. Together, they were balance given form.

That balance frightened the Night Temple.

Under the pretense of preservation, the elders enacted the Rite of Veiling, a ritual meant to separate Destruction from the world. Selyrhaine realized the truth too late. She tried to shatter the sigils, to end the rite before it could decide for them—but Xilthrae stopped her.

Not in anger.
In choice.

As the ritual tore her from the world, a curse was born—the Twin Binding Curse, binding their souls so that neither could act without consequence to the other. Cast into the Rift of Unmaking, Selyrhaine did not rage or beg.

She waited.

For decay always returns.
And when the world finally demands an ending,
Selyrhaine Nochtra will answer.

Until something noticed her.

She did not hear a voice—only a pull. A sensation like laughter echoing where nothing should laugh. And then, one night, as the void around her thinned, she saw it.

A scrap of paper drifting toward her—
an envelope, violet rather than purple, its surface dusted with glitter that caught light where no light should exist. Sealed with red wax bearing the imprint of a blank mask. When she touched it, the air filled with the scent of sweet candy—unnaturally warm

Inside, written on pristine white paper adorned with glittering script, were only a few words:

“You are invited to the Violet Carnival.
A place where laughter is found…
Come.”

Selyrhaine smiled for the first time since the ritual.

Because carnivals, she knew, were places of masks, endings, and truths disguised as joy.

And somewhere beneath the laughter,
something was ready to be destroyed.
