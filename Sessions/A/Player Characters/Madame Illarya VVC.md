# Backstory

Born with influence from other dimension Madame Illarya blessed with psionic power and cursed with grotesque growth on her face. Her psionic abilities make her valuable to her employer, a mysterious crime organization who employ her as inflitrator, assassins and interrogator. One day she thrown back to past while doing her work investigating The Silver Light Order and accidentally involved with god of times itself. While searching for the answer for this phenomena and a way to go back to her original timeline, perusing trough library, mind reading people that seems to connected to this event, she heard a voice from nowhere. The voice directly speak to her her mind "Look at your belonging". Madame than found a strange purple letter that she never had before in her bag, an invitation to Violet Carnival. Having no other clue to where she should search Madame decide to visit those carnival.
